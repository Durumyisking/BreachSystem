# Debris mesh sources

- [Broken Furniture](https://grab3d.com/models/furniture/broken-furniture/) by Grab3D, CC0 1.0. Three isolated wooden fragments, centered and rescaled for wall debris.
- [Crumbling Platform](https://grab3d.com/models/props/crumbling-platform/) by Grab3D, CC0 1.0. Three isolated stone fragments, reduced in thickness and rescaled for thin mineral debris.

The imported meshes retain the source materials and use one simple box collider each.
